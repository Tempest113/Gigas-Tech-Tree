    Reading the tree

    Every technology sits to the right of everything it needs, so any chain reads left to right. Rows are grouped into research categories.

    Columns

    Tier mode puts each technology in its research tier, with repeatables in a band at the far right. Within a tier, technologies behind an ascension perk sit right of those without one.

    Primary Gate mode groups by the ascension perk that locks a technology — No Gate, Mega-Engineering, Galactic Wonders, Gigastructural Constructs, Cosmogenesis — and orders by tier inside each band, so each band reads left to right like the tier view. Perks that themselves require Gigastructural Constructs (A Celestial Armada, A Weapon to Pierce the Heavens, Vast Expanses, Unshackled Transportation) are shown in that band rather than as gates of their own.

    The number under the name is research cost. T5 is the tier; ∞ marks an unlimited repeatable and ×40 one capped at 40 levels. A red line marks a dangerous technology.

    A badge on the icon marks a technology you cannot obtain without an ascension perk — whether the perk gates it or hands it over. A solid ring means this technology depends on the perk itself; a dashed ring means it sits behind one that does. The card names the perk, and the detail panel explains which case it is, including alternatives such as "requires Mechromancy or Genetic Ascension".

    Getting around

    • Click a technology to select it: its prerequisites and unlocks stay highlighted and everything else dims. Click empty space to clear.
    • Middle-click to show only that technology, what it needs and what it leads to, framed to fit — the quickest way to see a route to something. Esc or Clear restores the tree.
    • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, or use the controls at the bottom right.
    • On a touch screen drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a technology to select it and press and hold to isolate its chain — the equivalent of a middle-click. Filters live behind the Filters button in the header.
    • F search · Esc deselect · Backspace back

    Narrowing what you see

    The sidebar filters by category, by source and by requirement — "Requires Mega-Engineering" and the two perk filters cover both direct and inherited requirements. Unchecking a category removes its row entirely, and Hide all clears every category so a narrow view can be built up from nothing. The Explore tab lists everything as a sortable table.

    The view is in the address bar, so any selection, filter or isolated chain can be linked to someone.

    Where the data comes from

    Gigastructural Engineering & More merged over base Stellaris, the same way the game loads them: a card badged overrides vanilla replaces a base-game technology, and requires another mod means it only appears with that mod installed. The tree is rebuilt weekly from the mod's repository.
