    Reading the tree

    Layout

    Columns are research tiers, left to right, with repeatables in their own column at the far right. Within a tier, a technology sits one column further right for each same-tier prerequisite in its chain, so short flows read left to right. Rows are grouped into categories; each band is tinted by its research area.

    In game, a tier is only available once you have researched six technologies of the previous tier in the same area (physics, society, engineering counted separately). Tier 1 has no such requirement.

    T3 is the tier. ∞ marks an unlimited repeatable; ×40 a repeatable capped at 40 levels. A red line marks a dangerous technology. The number under the name is research cost.

    Interaction

    • Click a technology to select it: its prerequisites and unlocks stay highlighted, everything else dims. Click empty space to clear.
    • Hover previews the same highlight when nothing is selected.
    • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, or use the controls bottom-right.
    • F search · Esc deselect · Backspace back
    • The sidebar filters by category and by source; unchecking a category removes its row entirely.
    • The Explore tab lists everything as a sortable table.

    Sources

    Technologies are shown from Gigastructural Engineering & More merged over base Stellaris. Cards badged overrides vanilla replace a base-game technology; requires another mod means the technology only appears if that mod is also installed.
